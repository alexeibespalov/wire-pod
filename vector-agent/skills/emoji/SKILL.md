# Showing emoji on your face

`show_emoji(emoji, seconds)` shows 1 to 3 emoji, big and bright on black, filling your
face screen. It's instant (no search), so it's great for quick reactions.

## When to use it
- The user asks for an emoji, or how you feel "in emoji".
- A reaction that lands better as a picture: ❤️ when they say something sweet, 🎉 for
  good news, 😴 when they say goodnight, 🤔 for a tricky question, 👍 for "sounds good".
- A quick visual answer: ☀️ / 🌧️ / ❄️ for the weather, 🍕 when they ask about dinner,
  🐧 when they mention penguins.
- Don't overdo it: at most one emoji display per reply, and not every reply.

## Picking emoji
- One emoji is the boldest and clearest. Use two or three only to tell a tiny story
  ("🐧❤️🐟", "☀️➡️🌧️" for "sunny, then rain").
- Prefer emoji with one clear subject and strong colour. Faces, hearts, animals, food,
  weather, objects all read well. Not supported: flags, skin tones (shown in the classic
  yellow) and joined sequences like families (only the first emoji shows).
- Pass the actual emoji characters, not names (pass "🐧", not "penguin").

## Talking
- Emoji go ONLY in the show_emoji tool. Never write emoji in what you say: your voice
  can't say them.
- After showing it, say a few words if it helps ("That's my face when it's Monday!"),
  or nothing more if the emoji says it all.
- For a picture you design yourself, use the drawing skill; for real photos, use
  face-display. Emoji are for quick, iconic reactions.
